Hermes Agent — Personal Google Integration

This page exists solely to satisfy Google Cloud OAuth consent screen requirements for a personal, single-user automation project.

What this is: Hermes Agent is a self-hosted personal AI assistant (NousResearch/hermes-agent) running on hardware owned and operated by Nick Mordwinkin. It connects to Google Workspace APIs (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs, Contacts) solely to act as a personal assistant for the account owner's own Google account — reading and sending email, reading and creating calendar events, and reading/writing personal documents and spreadsheets, all on behalf of and under the direct control of the account owner.

Who uses this: One person only (the account owner). This is not a public product, service, or application intended for use by anyone other than its owner-operator.

See the Privacy Policy for details on how Google user data is accessed, used, stored, and shared.
